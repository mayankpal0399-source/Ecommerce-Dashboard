# Ecommerce-Dashboard
Ecommerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The Ecommerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze ecommerce sales performance, product categories, payment methods, state-wise sales, monthly profitability, and customer purchasing behavior.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify profitable product categories, and understand customer trends to support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

Monitor total sales amount and quantity sold.

Analyze product demand across different categories.

Understand customer payment method preferences.

Compare sales performance across states.

Track monthly profit trends and identify loss-making periods.

Identify top-performing sub-categories based on profit.

Analyze customer-wise sales contributions.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.

DAX – Calculated measures and KPI analysis.

Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and source data management.

📈 Dashboard Features

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Sales Amount: Approximately 102K.

Total Quantity Sold: Approximately 1K units.

2. Category Analysis

Visualizes quantity sold across Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture to understand product demand.

3. Payment Mode Analysis

Analyzes quantity distribution across COD, UPI, Debit Card, EMI, and Credit Card to understand customer payment preferences.

4. State-wise Sales Analysis

Displays sales amount by state, with a state filter to explore regional performance.

5. Monthly Profit Analysis

Tracks monthly profit and loss trends to identify profitable months and periods requiring further investigation.

6. Sub-Category Profitability

Compares profit across product sub-categories, including Printers, Tables, Sarees, Trousers, Chairs, and other products.

7. Customer-wise Sales Analysis

Highlights customer-level sales contributions to identify high-value customers and understand revenue distribution.

💡 Key Insights

Clothing contributes approximately 55.82% of total quantity sold, making it the leading category in the displayed analysis.

Electronics contributes approximately 25.39%, while Furniture accounts for approximately 18.79% of quantity sold.

COD represents approximately 45% of total quantity by payment mode, making it the most prominent payment method in the displayed chart.

The monthly profit chart shows both positive and negative profit periods, highlighting opportunities to investigate seasonality and profitability.

Printers and Tables appear among the leading sub-categories by profit in the displayed ranking.

The customer sales chart shows differences in customer contributions, supporting customer-level performance analysis.

Note: These insights reflect the dashboard visuals. Further validation against the underlying dataset is recommended before making business decisions.
